# S-21 — Make test procedures and common questions required

**Issue:** #89  **Commit:** (this commit)  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
`procedure` and `questions` are required on `CriterionRecord` (`src/criteria/types.ts`). The
data-integrity test (`tests/criteria.test.ts`, "every authored criterion record") fails any of
the 55 records with fewer than three steps, no common question, a repeated step or question, or
a blank step, question or answer. Every criterion page now always renders "How to test" and
"Common questions".

## Decisions made
- The minimum lives in the data-integrity test, next to the other completeness checks, as one
  `it.each` over `CORPUS`. The S-18 principle-1-only check in `tests/writing.test.ts` is gone;
  the writing test keeps only the prose rules.
- Checked it can fail: cutting 4.1.3 to two steps fails exactly that record's case.
- `CriterionPage` drops the `procedure?.length` / `questions?.length` guards; the two sections
  are unconditional. The h2-order test in `tests/criterionPage.test.tsx` expects both on every
  page rather than conditionally.
- `tests/filter.test.ts` builds bare records; they now carry `procedure: []`, `questions: []`
  (the filter never reads them).
- `recordProse` in `tests/writing.ts` still accepts any object shape on purpose (its own test
  feeds it a non-record shape), so it was left alone.
- CONTEXT.md and docs/CONVENTIONS.md now describe both fields as required.

## Deliberately left
- The S-17 literal h2-list test for 4.1.2/4.1.3 in `tests/criterionPage.test.tsx` stays; it
  will need "Further reading" once a post listing 4.1.2 lands (the post PRs own that fix).
- The acceptance checkbox in `docs/PRD-seo.md` is left unticked, as for earlier tickets.
