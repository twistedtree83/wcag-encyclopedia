# T-25 — Content: guideline 4.1 Compatible (2 criteria)

**Issue:** #25  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped

4.1.2 and 4.1.3 — and with them **the complete corpus: all 55 Level A and AA success criteria
of WCAG 2.2**, across all four principles and all thirteen guidelines. 19 carry a markup diff.

## Decisions made

**The completeness gate is now equality, in both directions.** T-02 shipped `corpus ⊆ manifest`
and deferred equality to this task, on the grounds that a failing assertion is worse than an
absent one while content is still landing. Four assertions now hold: nothing in the standard is
undocumented, nothing outside the closed set is documented, there are exactly 55 records, and
every one of the thirteen guidelines has at least one. **Adding a criterion that is not in WCAG
2.2, or dropping one that is, now breaks the build.**

**4.1.1 Parsing's absence is explained on the card, not just in the manifest.** Readers arriving
from a 2.1 checklist will look for it, and "it was removed because browsers recover from
malformed markup consistently and it was failing sites for problems no assistive technology
had" is the answer they need.

**4.1.2's example shows the accessibility tree output**, not the markup — because the criterion
is about what is *exposed*, and "announced as: `"" — group`" makes the failure concrete in a way
a `div` soup listing does not.

**4.1.3's card points at this page's own filter count**, which is a `role="status"` live region,
and at the scroll-position strip, which deliberately is *not* one. Having both examples on the
same page is the clearest statement of when to use it and when not to.

## Deliberately left

- The `Not yet documented` branch in `GuidelineSection` is now unreachable, since the
  completeness test guarantees every guideline has criteria. Kept as graceful degradation if a
  future WCAG version adds a guideline to the structure ahead of its content.
- One e2e test (`distinguishes "filtered out" from "not yet documented"`) now skips itself,
  by design — it asserts a state the completed corpus no longer has.
- 4.1.2 and 4.1.3 should gain `demo:` links when **T-12** lands.
