## Decisions made

**Screen reader output is drawn as visible speech bubbles.** No synthesised audio anywhere, and
none is needed — what these criteria turn on is *what* gets announced, which reads perfectly
well. The 4.1.2 demo's whole argument is three buttons that all announce "button", which is more
damning on the page than it would be through a speaker.

**The 2.5.8 fingertip is drawn to scale.** A 45px contact patch against 18px and then 24px
targets, positioned by the timeline. The reader sees the patch covering three controls at once,
which is the fact the criterion encodes.

**The 2.2.2 demo does not itself violate 2.2.2.** Its carousel advances only as the reader
scrubs or plays, and it inherits the player's `prefers-reduced-motion` handling — so a demo
about unstoppable motion is fully stoppable. This was in the issue's acceptance criteria and is
worth restating: it would have been easy to autoplay a real carousel inside the stage and
reintroduce the exact failure on the card teaching it.

**The 4.1.3 demo shows the live region existing before it is filled**, since that ordering is
the part people get wrong — a region injected together with its text announces nothing in many
screen readers.
