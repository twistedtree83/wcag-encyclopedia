## What shipped
Wiring only. The lab-coverage skip-list (`tests/labsToCome.ts`) is deleted, and `uncoveredCriteria(records)` in `src/labs/registry.ts` no longer takes a to-come list. `tests/criteria.test.ts` now asserts, with no exceptions: all 55 criteria are in the corpus and each has at least one lab; every criterion has live fail and pass examples (replacing the "4.1.2 pilot has one" check); the flagship set is exactly the ten in #40.

## Decisions made
- The live-example completeness is asserted at the data level as well as by `e2e/live-examples.spec.ts` ("every criterion page swaps in its live examples"), so a record that loses its live version fails vitest before the browser run.
- Flagship URL state: each of the ten has its query module listed under "URL contracts" in `docs/CONVENTIONS.md`, and its states are axe-scanned in both themes and at 320px (in `e2e/axe.spec.ts` STATE_PAGES or the lab's own spec: colour-check, reflow, text-fit, target-size, timing-live). No new state pages were needed.
- Docs: CONVENTIONS "Labs" drops the "delete your line from labsToCome" step and states the no-gap rule; PRD "Coverage" notes the list was removed once it emptied.
