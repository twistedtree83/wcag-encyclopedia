# L-15 — Orientation and images of text: 1.3.4 and 1.4.5

**Issue:** #55  **Commit:** see `git log --grep "L-15:"` on `labs`  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
1.3.4 has live examples: an order page on a phone in the zoom frame. The Broken page is locked to portrait and the Fixed page adapts. A new "Rotate" key and an "Orientation" instrument drive them. 1.3.4 also has a lab, "Try it: turn the phone", where the reader holds the phone portrait or landscape and sets the page to adapt or to lock either way. 1.4.5 has live examples: a sale heading that is a real raster image (Broken) or real text (Fixed), driven by "Zoom in" and a new "Use my colours" key, with a "Zoom and your colours" instrument. 1.4.5's lab is "Try it: zoom and restyle the heading", where the reader picks the heading, the zoom and the colours.

## Decisions made
- **The orientation lock is real CSS.** `.opage` is its own size container, and `@container (orientation: …)` hides `.opage__body` and shows the `[data-rotate-prompt]` element. The stage measures whether that prompt is drawn (`getClientRects`) after the frame re-lays out, and pushes `{ kind: 'rotate', turned, width, height, hidden }` (`rotateSaid`).
- **The phone base is portrait** (`PHONE` 360×640 in `orientation.ts`), so the frame's `turned` means landscape here. ZoomFrame now also exposes `data-orientation="portrait|landscape"` (one additive line). CSS draws a portrait frame narrow (`width: min(100%, 14rem)`).
- **The 1.3.4 verdict fails whenever the page is locked**, even when the phone is held the way the page allows, because a reader who can't turn the phone is locked out either way. The readout says "Content shown, but only this way up". It starts Portrait + Adapts, which passes. Keyboard drive: page radio ↓.
- **The image of text is a real PNG**, drawn on a canvas at 1x after mount (`useHeadlineImage` in `ImagesOfTextLab.tsx`), so zoom genuinely stretches its pixels. The sharpness readout comes from the model: at zoom Z each pixel is stretched Z/100 times. The 1.4.5 verdict fails for the image at any zoom.
- **"Use my colours"** wraps the example in `ReaderColours` (`.reader-colours--on`: `--on-a` on `--a2` and Verdana, with `!important`, like a user stylesheet). Only stages with the `restyle` key get the wrapper. Those stages push a `{ kind: 'look', zoom, restyled, image }` event after every zoom and every restyle (`lookSaid`). `image` is whether the example holds an `img`.
- New keys: `rotate` ("Rotate") and `restyle` ("Use my colours"). New instruments: `orientation-meter` and `text-look`. Neither lab is a flagship, so neither has URL state.
