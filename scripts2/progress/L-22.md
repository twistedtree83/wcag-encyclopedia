# L-22 — Timing, audio and flashing: 2.2.2, 1.4.2 and 2.3.1

**Issue:** #62  **Commit:** see `git log --grep "L-22:"` on `labs`  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
Live examples on both stages, on L-21's clock, for three criteria. 2.2.2: a carousel that moves a slide every four mock seconds, with no control (Broken, inert) or a real Pause button first (Fixed). 1.4.2: a track that plays as the page loads, drawn as a level meter because the site is silent, with no control or a real "Pause audio" first. 2.3.1: a banner set to flash 8 times a second (Broken) or not at all (Fixed), depicted as one second of still frames, with a flash-rate meter that counts on the clock. Three labs: "Try it: stop the carousel" (2.2.2), "Try it: quiet the autoplay" (1.4.2), "Try it: raise the flash rate" (2.3.1).

## Decisions made
- **2.3.1 never flashes.** The banner is a still 16-frame film strip (`FlashStrip`) and the rate is a bar against a limit line (`FlashMeter`, `src/labs/timingParts.tsx`). Nothing in the L-22 CSS block animates. `e2e/timing-flash.spec.ts` samples every element's luminance (background, text, border, fill, times opacity) every 50 ms while both clocks run and the lab sits at 8 a second, and asserts no element makes more than three changes (runs in one direction, so a transition counts once) in any one second, and no looping animation exists.
- **Generic clock story** `src/labs/clockStory.ts` (`StoryBeat` with `ends: 'bad' | 'ok'`, `storyAt`, `storyEnd`, `storySaid(story, before, now, still, quiet)`), generalising `timeout.ts`. 2.2.1's lab was left on its own copy (out of scope).
- **The 2.2.2 and 1.4.2 labs wait 2 seconds, not 20**: their stories play out in seconds (`useClockStory` in `TimingLabs.tsx`). The 2.3.1 lab has no clock: the rate is drawn, never played.
- **Verdicts.** 2.2.2 passes with a pause, stop or hide control, or motion that stops by itself inside 5 seconds. 1.4.2 passes with pause or volume at the top, or a track under 3 seconds. 2.3.1 passes at 3 a second or fewer (the small-area and red allowances are left to the restatement). The 2.2.2 and 1.4.2 labs start failing; 2.3.1 starts at 2 a second and the reader raises it.
- `data-timer` names a phase (moving / past the threshold / paused), never the slide or level, so a running clock is quiet.
- The 2.2.2 demo (`autoplay-2-2-2`) is unchanged; the e2e checks it still shows beside the lab.

## Deliberately left
- No URL contract for these labs (the issue did not ask for one).
- Reader-driven rapid changes (holding an arrow key on the rate radios) change the small meter bar per keypress; that is the reader's own input on a small area and is not sampled.
