# S-20 — Test procedures and common questions: Understandable

**Issue:** #88  **Commit:** (this commit)  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
All 13 Understandable criteria (3.1.1, 3.1.2, 3.2.1, 3.2.2, 3.2.3, 3.2.4, 3.2.6, 3.3.1, 3.3.2,
3.3.3, 3.3.4, 3.3.7, 3.3.8) carry a `procedure` and three `questions`, in
`src/criteria/g3-1.tsx`, `g3-2.tsx` and `g3-3.tsx`. Principle 4 was already complete (S-17).

## Decisions made
- Five steps each, except 3.2.6 and 3.3.2, which have four: a fifth step would have repeated
  one already there. Every record has at least the three steps S-21 will require.
- Every answer was checked against the W3C Understanding document for its criterion
  (w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/<slug>.html) and restated, not copied. Points that rest on
  it: 3.1.1 declares the main language, or the first when two are used equally; 3.2.4 asks for
  consistent, not identical, labels, and covers two copies on one page; 3.2.6 compares
  serialised order, not pixels, and does not require help; 3.3.2 is not met by aria-label alone;
  3.3.4 needs one of its three options, and a checkbox beside the submit button (G155) is
  sufficient; 3.3.7 is not met by browser autofill and covers one session; 3.3.8 allows codes
  that can be pasted, and excepts object recognition and personal content at AA only.
- Answers that go a step past the Understanding text, for the owner's review: 3.1.1 "change
  lang to match" when an app switches language; 3.3.4 "a newsletter sign-up is usually
  outside it" (the page says only that plain creation or editing of data is not the target);
  3.3.3's step on a wrong password, which follows this page's own example note rather than the
  Understanding document, which names only security and purpose.
- Apostrophes inside single-quoted strings are typographic (’), as in the rest of the corpus.
- No new test: the ticket sets no check, and `tests/writing.test.ts` already reads every
  record's procedure and questions.
- `tests/styleRules.test.ts` also reads these strings: it bans "rule" for a criterion or a
  threshold anywhere in a criterion record, so "a field with a rule" or "the test 3.3.8 rules
  out" fail. `tests/writing.test.ts` and `tests/criterionPage.test.tsx` alone do not catch
  it; run `tests/styleRules.test.ts` with them while drafting.
