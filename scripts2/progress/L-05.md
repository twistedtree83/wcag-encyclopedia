# L-05 — Keyboard: 2.1.1, 2.1.2 and 2.1.4

**Issue:** #45  **Commit:** see `git log --grep "L-05:"` on `labs`  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
2.1.1, 2.1.2 and 2.1.4 now have live examples on both stages and one lab each. 2.1.1: a Quantity field and "Add to basket". On Broken the control is a click-only div, so simulated Tab only ever lands on the field (the focus tracker shows one stop); on Fixed it is a button that real Tab, Enter and Space reach and press, and the basket count is a status message. 2.1.2: a newsletter panel that loops focus. On Broken, Tab cycles Email and Subscribe, Escape does nothing and Close is a mouse-only span; on Fixed the loop is real but Escape and a real Close button shut it and focus returns to a "Sign up for the newsletter" trigger. 2.1.4: a message ("s" stars, "e" archives) and a Reply box. A new "Dictate “send this”" key sends the phrase letter by letter: on Broken the shortcuts listen on the whole view and fire; on Fixed they listen only on the focused message. The labs: "reach the button by keyboard" (div / div with tabindex / button, with simulated Tab, Enter, Space), "trap the focus, or let it go" (trap / trap with Escape exit / no trap, drawn inside the mock, with a labelled "Take focus out of the widget" that always works) and "dictate past a shortcut" (always on / remapped to Alt+E / focus only).

## Decisions made
- **New keys:** `SimKey` gains `escape` ("Escape") and `dictate` ("Dictate “send this”", phrase in `DICTATION` in `LiveStage.tsx`). Both dispatch real `keydown` events to whatever has focus (simulated or real), or to the example's first element when nothing does. A letter the example doesn't cancel is appended to the field it lands on. Dispatching works inside the inert fail stage.
- **Actions seam:** `src/live/actions.ts` `didAction(el, text)` fires a bubbling `live-action` CustomEvent from an example's own handler. The stage collects them during the bar's dispatch, and on the pass stage also from real keys (remembering the last real key name).
- **New event + instrument:** `StageEvent` `keys` (`keys`, `dictated`, `target`, `actions`); instrument `key-log` ("Keys": the keys, "Focus: …", then each action or "Nothing happened"). `keyLines` / `keysSaid` in `events.ts`; the polite region says `keysSaid` on both stages, since the bar did it.
- **Focus tracker:** the trail restarts when a focus event's `total` differs from the previous one (the example's Tab stops changed, e.g. the 2.1.2 panel closed). Otherwise trail labels would point at stops that no longer exist.
- **2.1.2 pass stage traps real focus, with an exit.** That is what the caption ("The loop has an exit") and 2.1.2 itself allow: the exit (Escape, Close) is written on the panel. The **lab** never traps real focus; e2e tabs out of it in all three modes.
- Lab models are pure: `src/labs/keyboard.ts`, `keyboardTrap.ts`, `shortcuts.ts`. All three labs start failing; no URL state (not flagships). A drawn ring in lab mocks is `.lab__mock .is-focus`.
- Shared CSS in the swatch.css "L-05" section; `.stage__instruments > .ftrack + .alog` gives the log below the tracker a gap.

## Deliberately left
- Real speech input is out of scope; dictation is simulated on both stages.
- The 2.1.4 lab dictates with focus in the reply box only; a "focus on the message" toggle wasn't needed for the verdict.
