# L-21 — Clock and the 2.2.1 flagship

**Issue:** #61  **Commit:** see `git log --grep "L-21:"` on `labs`  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
A new instrument, the clock (`src/live/clock.ts`): mock time that never touches the reader's real session and never runs on load. On a live stage the bar gains "Wait 20 seconds" and "Run the clock" (which becomes "Pause the clock"); the `clock` instrument shows the mock time and each change the example made on it. 2.2.1 now has live examples on both stages: a two-minute application that ends silently and clears the answer (Broken, inert), and one that warns at 1:40 in an alert with "Give me more time", and keeps the answer if time runs out (Fixed, real). It also has the flagship lab "Try it: time out the session": the reader picks the limit and what the site offers, then runs the clock on Sam, who needs four minutes.

## Decisions made
- **Pure clock, stage-owned state.** `waited`/`ticked`/`toggled` are pure and stop at `end`; `useTicker` runs an interval only while running. A running clock is 10x real speed (2 mock s per 200 ms). Reduced motion needs nothing extra: the clock is stopped until started in every mode, and its state changes are text.
- **Examples read the time from context** (`ClockNow`, `useClockNow()`), derive what they show in render, and name it in `data-timer`. The stage reads `data-timer` in a layout effect after each clock move and pushes a `ClockEvent`; a change the example makes itself (the extension) is caught by a MutationObserver as cause `example` and not said.
- **Quiet ticks coalesce** (`withClock`), so a running clock doesn't crowd focus events out of the stage's 40-event buffer.
- **Lab verdict is the criterion's, not Sam's outcome.** A 5-minute limit with no warning lets Sam finish and still fails; a 5-second warning fails (Sam needs 10 s to react). Passes: 20 s warning with More time, a ten-times-longer limit, or turning it off. Starts failing (2 minutes, no warning); the keyboard drive is ↓↓ on "Before it ends".
- **The lab's story is pure** (`src/labs/timeout.ts`, `storyOf` → beats); the clock stops at the last beat; a choice change sets the clock back to 0:00. Ticks announce only beats (`storySaid(..., quiet)`). Clock buttons are never disabled, so focus isn't dropped at the end.
- URL contract `?limit=`, `?help=` in `timeoutQuery.ts` (CONVENTIONS). The clock is never in the URL.
- How-to: `notes/clock.md` (orchestrator notes).
