# S-14 — Post: Colour contrast beyond text

**Issue:** #82  **Commit:** see the PR (branch `post/s-14-contrast`)  **Status:** shipped, waiting on the owner's approval of its PR

## What shipped
One post, `src/blog/posts/colour-contrast-beyond-text.md` (`/blog/colour-contrast-beyond-text/`),
published 2026-11-28. It explains what a contrast ratio measures (with one worked luminance
calculation), then takes 1.4.3, 1.4.11 and 1.4.1 in turn, then shows all three on one email
field in an error state, and ends with a five-step check. It embeds the `colour-check` lab for
1.4.3, the `non-text-contrast` lab for 1.4.11 and the 1.4.1 examples (`::example{num="1.4.1"}`).

## Decisions made
- Slug `colour-contrast-beyond-text`: the ticket's own title, tied to no version or year, so it
  never needs to change.
- Criteria: 1.4.1, 1.4.3, 1.4.11 only. 2.4.7 (focus ring visibility) is mentioned without a
  name or link, to keep "Further reading" off a page the post says little about; S-15 covers it.
  1.4.6 (AAA) is not mentioned: it is outside the corpus, so it has no page to link.
- Every ratio was computed with the WCAG 2.2 relative-luminance formula (0.04045 threshold):
  #767676 4.54, #777777 4.48, #949494 3.03, #959595 2.995, #a9a9a9 2.35, #cccccc 1.61,
  #4c9ffe 2.73, #0b57d0 6.39, #2b2b2b 14.16, #0057b8 6.87, #2f6fe0 4.70, #d93025 4.77,
  #188038 5.02 (each on white); white on #0078d4 4.53; #2b2b2b vs #0057b8 2.06, vs #2f6fe0 3.02;
  #d93025 vs #188038 1.05.
- Facts checked against Understanding 1.4.3, 1.4.11 and 1.4.1, technique G183, the WCAG 2.2
  definitions, "What's New in WCAG 2.1" and Chrome's Rendering-tab documentation. No new source
  domain.
- The same two unit-test fixes as S-11 to S-13, applied by hand because `seo` has moved on since
  PR #92 (S-17 added sections to the same test): `tests/criterionPage.test.tsx` expects "Further
  reading" when `postsAbout(num, POSTS)` is non-empty, and `tests/share.test.ts` counts one card
  per post. A merge conflict with the other post PRs resolves to either side.
