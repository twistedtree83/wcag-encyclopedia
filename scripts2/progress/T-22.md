**Issue:** #22  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped

3.1.1, 3.1.2. Corpus is 42 of 55; guideline 3.1 complete.

## Decisions made

**Both examples are framed around what a screen reader actually does with the attribute**,
because the cost of omitting it is invisible to anyone reading with their eyes. "English read
with German pronunciation rules is unintelligible" is the concrete consequence; "declare your
language" is not.

**3.1.2's pass example marks up real French inside a real sentence**, with `lang="fr"` on the
element — so the page is itself doing the thing it documents, and a reader with a screen reader
open hears the voice switch on this very card.

**Both cards name the exemption**, since over-application is the common failure mode of 3.1.2:
words absorbed into the surrounding language ("café", "rendezvous") need nothing, and tagging
them makes the reading worse.

## Deliberately left

3.1.3–3.1.6 (unusual words, abbreviations, reading level, pronunciation) are AAA.
