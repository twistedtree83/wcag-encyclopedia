# S-17 — Test procedures and common questions: the record shape and their sections

**Issue:** #85  **Commit:** (this commit)  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
`procedure` and `questions` join the criterion record (`src/criteria/types.ts`), optional until
S-21. The criterion page renders "How to test" (an ordered list) and "Common questions" (an h3
per question, its answer in a `p` under it) when present. 4.1.2 and 4.1.3 carry both: five steps
and three questions each. The writing test reaches both fields on a real record.

## Decisions made
- **Shape:** `procedure?: readonly string[]`; `questions?: readonly CommonQuestion[]` with
  `CommonQuestion = { question: string; answer: string }`. Plain text only, no JSX: the writing
  test reads strings, and the page renders them as text. Attribute names go in as written
  (`role="status"`, `aria-live="assertive"`); they render as plain text, not code.
- **Place:** after the demos ("Watch it happen") and before the labs ("Try it yourself"), so
  the full order is Broken then fixed, diff, demos, How to test, Common questions, labs,
  (S-09: Further reading), Also in. h2 ids `procedure-title` and `questions-title`.
- "How to test" has a one-line intro naming the criterion number; "Common questions" has none.
- Each step and each question is the React key, so they must be unique within a record.
- CSS: `.procedure`, `.questions`, `.questions__item` in `swatch.css`, section "Criterion page:
  how to test and common questions (S-17)", 64ch wide.
- No `FAQPage` or `HowTo` JSON-LD: the PRD rules both out.
- Writing rules that apply to every step, question and answer: every banned construction in
  WRITING.md (case-insensitive), at most one em dash per string, no three-adjective lists. No
  length rule, no source rule. Review-only: British spelling, a step is one action plus what to
  look for (browser and a named free tool), an answer starts with "Yes, …", "No, …" or
  "Only when …", and each answer is checked against the W3C Understanding document.

## Deliberately left
- Content for the other 53 criteria: S-18 (Perceivable), S-19 (Operable), S-20 (Understandable).
  4.1.1 is gone, so 4.1.2 and 4.1.3 are the whole of principle 4.
- Making both fields required (at least three steps, one question): S-21.
- No e2e of its own: the per-route axe scan (both themes, 320px) and the h2-order unit test
  cover the two pages.
