# S-12 — Post: The European Accessibility Act and WCAG

**Issue:** #80  **Commit:** see `git log -- src/blog/posts/european-accessibility-act-and-wcag.md`  **Status:** shipped (awaiting owner approval on its PR)

## What shipped
The post `src/blog/posts/european-accessibility-act-and-wcag.md` (slug
`european-accessibility-act-and-wcag`, permanent). It follows the chain from Directive (EU)
2019/882 to EN 301 549 to WCAG Level AA, gives the Act's dates, scope, exceptions and
transitional periods, and shows the six criteria WCAG 2.2 adds at A and AA, with the 2.4.11
example and the `target-size` lab embedded. It says plainly that it is not legal advice.

## Decisions made
- `etsi.org` joins `SOURCE_DOMAINS` (Standards) in `tests/writing.ts`, and the Standards line of
  `docs/WRITING.md`, so EN 301 549 itself can be cited.
- EUR-Lex answers automated fetches with a bot challenge, which was not bypassed. The Directive
  was read in full from the Publications Office's public content-negotiation endpoint
  (`https://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/32019L0882`, the same Official Journal text);
  the post cites the EUR-Lex ELI link, the canonical address.
- EN 301 549 V4.1.1 (2026-09) was read from ETSI's PDF: foreword (C(2022) 6456, Annex ZB,
  presumption once cited), adoption date 24 August 2026, clause 9.0 (WCAG 2.2 AA = 9.1–9.4 +
  9.6; WCAG 2.1 AA = the same less six clauses), clause 9.7 (user preferences, beyond WCAG).
- Whether V4.1.1 is cited: AccessibleEU's 7 September 2026 news item says not yet, and that
  V3.2.1 (WCAG 2.1 AA) remains the current reference. The post attributes that to AccessibleEU
  and tells the reader to check the Official Journal.
- Two tests assumed no real post exists, and failed once one did. Fixed deliberately, not
  weakened: `tests/criterionPage.test.tsx` now expects "Further reading" when
  `postsAbout(num, POSTS)` is non-empty, and `tests/share.test.ts` counts one card per post as
  well as per criterion (S-07 gives every post its own card). Any other launch post that merges
  first makes the same fix; the second to merge takes either copy.
- Criteria: 2.4.11, 2.5.7, 2.5.8, 3.2.6, 3.3.7, 3.3.8 (the six new A/AA criteria in WCAG 2.2).

## Deliberately left

  added (`/blog/<slug>/` links are not accepted by the parser yet).
- If the Commission cites EN 301 549 V4.1.1 in the Official Journal, the "Which version
  applies" section needs an `updated` date and a rewrite.
