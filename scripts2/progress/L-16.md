# L-16 — Vision filter, with 1.4.1, 1.4.11, 1.3.3 and the 1.4.3 live examples

**Issue:** #56  **Commit:** see `git log --grep "L-16:"` on `labs`  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
A new instrument, the vision filter: a "Vision filter" key on the input bar puts greyscale or a colour-vision simulation (no red, green or blue cones) on the example, and the instrument under the stage measures each cue whose colour carries meaning through that filter and says whether it still stands out. 1.4.1, 1.4.11, 1.4.3 and 1.3.3 have live examples on both stages. Three new labs: "take the colour away" (1.4.1: underline the links, add an icon to the error), "fade the edges" (1.4.11: a field and checkbox edge faded past 3:1) and "rewrite the instruction" (1.3.3, its first lab). The colour check and fade-the-text labs are unchanged.

## Decisions made
- **One model, two renderers.** `src/live/vision.ts` holds the matrices and measures; `VisionDefs.tsx` draws with the same matrices. Greyscale is CSS `grayscale(1)` (sRGB, fades over 0.2s as the colour preview does, the design guide's existing filter exception); the colour-vision views are SVG `feColorMatrix` in linearRGB (Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes 2009, severity 1) and switch at once. So every ratio shown is the contrast engine's, on the colours on screen.
- **Cues are marked in markup** (`data-cue`, `-part`, `-vs`, `-need`, `-also`; `src/live/cues.ts`) and read from `getComputedStyle` when the key is pressed, so live ratios follow the theme. A cue "stands out" if it clears `need` against its reference or has a non-colour mark (an underline is detected; others are declared).
- **1.4.1's broken links are spans**, as in the static example: axe's `link-in-text-block` checks inert content, so real hue-only links would fail the page scan. 1.4.1's bar is just "Vision filter".
- **1.4.1's live sentence is `--ink-2` (`.vis-prose`)**: against `--ink` the light-theme link is 3.11:1 and would technically clear the 3:1 link rule, contradicting the caption.
- **New tokens** `ex-go`, `ex-stop`, `ex-on-button` (1.3.3's buttons, same in both themes; no literal hex in CSS). `TOKEN_PAIRS` gained card-bg rows for the ex-* tokens, since live examples sit on `--card-bg`.
- The 1.4.1 lab judges its verdict in greyscale (only cues that survive without hue count). The 1.4.11 lab reuses the "Borders and icons" row from `thresholds.ts`. The 1.3.3 lab passes when the instruction names the button, even if it also gives colour and place.
- `tests/labShell.test.tsx`: 1.4.1 and 1.4.11 now show two labs before hydration; the expected verdict lists were updated deliberately.
- How-to: notes `vision-filter.md` in the run's scratchpad (API, simulated vs real, gotchas).

## Deliberately left
- The existing colour check and fade-the-text labs are untouched, per the issue.
- No initial cue readout before the first press (the instrument says to press the key); adding one would need a non-reader event at mount.
