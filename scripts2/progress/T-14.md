## Decisions made

**Every example depicts a player; none embeds one.** This page ships no `<video>` or `<audio>`
element by design and an e2e test enforces it. That turns out not to be a constraint worth
fighting: what these criteria actually turn on is what sits *around* the media — captions,
transcripts, description tracks — not the media itself. A shared `Player` mock renders the
screen, an optional caption bar, and a transport.

**The examples separate criteria that are easily conflated.** 1.2.2 vs 1.2.3 is the pair people
get wrong: captions carry what was *said*, descriptions carry what was *shown*. The 1.2.3 fail
example is a caption that is perfectly accurate and still useless — "Then you just do this" —
because the information was in the gesture. 1.2.5 then shows why a described transcript that
satisfies 1.2.3 at Level A does not satisfy 1.2.5 at AA.

**1.2.2's failing example is auto-captions, not missing captions.** Missing captions are
obvious; uncorrected machine captions are the realistic failure, and they fail on exactly the
words the sentence depends on.

### A test rule that was wrong

The diff test asserted every diff must contain **both** a removal and an addition. 1.2.5's real
fix is purely additive — adding a `<track kind="descriptions">` — and manufacturing a removal to
satisfy the test would have been dishonest. The rule is now "at least one non-context line",
which is the actual invariant it was reaching for. **Later content tasks: a purely additive
diff is fine.**

## Deliberately left

Nothing in 1.2. AAA criteria in this guideline (1.2.6 through 1.2.9 — sign language, extended
descriptions, live audio-only) are outside the closed set.
