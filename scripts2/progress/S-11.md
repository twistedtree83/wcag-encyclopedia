# S-11 — Post: What's new in WCAG 2.2

**Issue:** #79  **Commit:** see `git log --grep "S-11"`  **Status:** shipped (awaiting the owner's approval on its PR)

## What shipped
The first real post, `src/blog/posts/whats-new-in-wcag-2-2.md`, at `/blog/whats-new-in-wcag-2-2/`.
It covers the six Level A and AA criteria new in WCAG 2.2 (what fails, the fix, how to check),
names the three AAA ones with links to W3C, explains why 4.1.1 Parsing was removed and where
its real harms now fail (1.3.1, 4.1.2), and ends with how to move a 2.1 audit to 2.2. It embeds
the `sticky-banner` lab (2.4.11) and the 2.5.8 examples.

## Decisions made
- Slug `whats-new-in-wcag-2-2` is permanent: the feed entry id is built from it.
- `criteria` lists the six new criteria plus 1.3.1 and 4.1.2, because the 4.1.1 section names
  them as where broken-markup harm now fails. That puts the post under "Further reading" on all
  eight pages; the owner may want to drop 1.3.1 and 4.1.2.
- AAA criteria (2.4.12, 2.4.13, 3.3.9, 2.5.5) have no criterion page, so they link to their W3C
  Understanding documents in the body; they are not in `criteria`.
- No new source domain was needed: every source is on w3.org.
- Facts checked against the WCAG 2.2 Recommendation (12 December 2024 edition), What's New in
  WCAG 2.2, the WCAG 2 FAQ and the Understanding documents for each criterion and for Parsing.
  The house view (records in `src/criteria/g2-4.tsx`, `g2-5.tsx`, `g3-2.tsx`, `g3-3.tsx`,
  `g4-1.tsx`) agrees with them.
