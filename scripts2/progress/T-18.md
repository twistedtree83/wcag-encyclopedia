## Decisions made

**2.2.1's pass example saves the work as well as warning**, and the caption says plainly that
saving is not required by the criterion — it is what separates compliant from kind. Worth
distinguishing so a reader does not mistake the extra for the requirement.

**2.2.2's diff shows both halves of the fix.** `prefers-reduced-motion` decides whether the
carousel starts; the pause button decides whether it continues. Shipping only the media query
leaves a reader with no stated preference stuck; shipping only the button ignores one that was
stated. The note says so explicitly.

**The examples are static depictions, not moving carousels.** A page documenting 2.2.2 must not
itself autoplay motion alongside other content — it would fail the criterion on the card that
teaches it. The moving version belongs in T-12's demo, where the reader controls playback.
