# S-18 — Test procedures and common questions: Perceivable

**Issue:** #86  **Commit:** (this commit)  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
All 20 Perceivable criteria (1.1.1 to 1.4.13, in `src/criteria/g1-1.tsx` to `g1-4.tsx`) carry a
`procedure` (4 to 6 steps) and `questions` (3 or 4). The criterion pages now render "How to
test" and "Common questions" for each. A new case in `tests/writing.test.ts` holds every
principle-1 record to at least 3 distinct steps and 1 distinct question.

## Decisions made
- **Check per principle:** `criteriaInPrinciple(1)` in `tests/writing.test.ts`, at S-21's
  minimum (3 steps, 1 question) plus uniqueness, because steps and questions are React keys.
  S-21 can replace it with one check over the whole `CORPUS`.
- **Fields go last in each record**, after `diff`, as on 4.1.2 and 4.1.3.
- **Sources:** each answer was checked against the W3C Understanding document for its
  criterion (WCAG 2.2), fetched on 2026-11-28; the 1.4.1 link answer also against technique
  G183. Facts relied on: G183 accepts colour-only inline links at 3:1 against surrounding text
  (no hover cue in the technique itself); the 1.2.4 note that two-way calls in web apps are out
  of scope; G203 (talking-head video) as the one text-only case for 1.2.5; extended
  description (G8) is sufficient for 1.2.5 but required only by 1.2.7; F100 ("please rotate")
  fails 1.3.4; placeholder text is in scope of 1.4.3 and ratios are not rounded; the
  Understanding page for 1.3.1 does not make skipped heading levels a failure; F46 for `th` in
  layout tables; 1.4.11 says focus indicators need 3:1; `title` tooltips are exempt from 1.4.13
  and Escape is an example, not a requirement.
- **Not stated by W3C, so left out:** whether automatic captions are enough (1.2.2, 1.2.4),
  `autocomplete="off"` (1.3.5), and click-triggered content (1.4.13). None of these has a
  question.
- Named free tools: the browser accessibility inspector, DevTools colour picker and Rendering
  panel, Firefox No Style and Zoom Text Only, WAVE, HeadingsMap, Colour Contrast Analyser,
  Stylus or a text spacing bookmarklet, NVDA and VoiceOver.

## Deliberately left
- Operable and Understandable content: S-19 and S-20. Making the fields required: S-21.
- No e2e of its own: the per-route axe scan covers the 20 pages.

## Test changed on purpose
`tests/furtherReading.test.tsx` hard-coded the h2 list of 1.4.3 as it was before 1.4.3 had a
procedure. It now expects How to test and Common questions between the examples and the labs,
which is the order S-17 set.
