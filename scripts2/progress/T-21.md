## Decisions made

**The guideline is framed around bodies rather than devices.** Each of these criteria quietly
assumes a steady hand, two working fingers, the ability to shake a phone, or the ability to hold
a drag without slipping. Naming that once at the top makes the six feel like one idea instead of
six unrelated rules.

**Every pass example keeps the original interaction.** Gestures stay, dragging stays,
shake-to-undo stays. None of these criteria asks you to remove an input method — they ask you to
stop making it the only one. That is the most commonly misread thing about 2.5, so all four
relevant cards say it explicitly.

**2.5.2's note names the actual cause**: reimplementing buttons on `pointerdown` to feel
snappier. A real `click` handler on a real button passes by default, so the criterion is almost
only ever failed by going out of one's way.

**2.5.3 explains why the well-meaning `aria-label` breaks things.** The best fix is usually no
`aria-label` at all.

**2.5.8's targets are rendered at their real sizes** — 16px versus 24px — rather than described,
so the difference is felt. The diff note distinguishes the 24px floor from the 44–48px platform
guidance this page uses for its own controls.
