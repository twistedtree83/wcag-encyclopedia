# L-18 — Pointer: 2.5.1, 2.5.2, 2.5.4 and 2.5.7

**Issue:** #58  **Commit:** see `git log --grep "L-18:"` on `labs`  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
Live examples on both stages and a lab for each of Pointer Gestures, Pointer Cancellation, Motion Actuation and Dragging Movements, all under the gesture pad. 2.5.1 is a harbour map that zooms by pinch, with + and − only on Fixed. 2.5.2 is a Delete draft button that acts on pointer down (Broken) or on click (Fixed). 2.5.4 is an inbox row with Archive where shaking undoes; Fixed adds Undo and a "Shake to undo" switch. 2.5.7 is a delivery list reordered by dragging a handle; Fixed adds move up and move down buttons. Four labs ("Try it: give the map buttons", "slide off the button", "shake to undo", "reorder without dragging") switch a drawn mock between the failing and passing design. They all start failing.

## Decisions made
- **New bar keys** (`SimKey`, labels in `LiveStage`): `pinch` ("Pinch"), `slide-off` ("Press and slide off"), `drag` ("Drag the top row down"), `shake` ("Shake the phone"). Each is one press. The pad never needs a drag or a path (2.5.1, 2.5.7).
- **The bar sends real pointer events** (`sendPointer` in LiveStage): touch, `isPrimary: false`, so the stage's recorder of the reader's own pointer ignores them and the example's own handlers run, inert or not. "Tap" now sends pointerdown and pointerup before `click()`, so an example acting on pointer down acts then. A down and up on the same target counts as a click; the bar never clicks after a slide-off. A tap with no targets lands mid-example and is said as "Tap landed on nothing".
- **Gesture model** (`gesture.ts`): `GestureName` gains `slide`, `drag`, `pinch`, `shake`. `GestureEvent` gains optional `path2` (a pinch's second finger) and `early` (how many actions fired before the pointer came up). Real pointers on the pass stage fill `early` too. Lines put early actions between "Pointer down" and "Pointer up". A pinch is said as "Two fingers spread apart / pinched together" and a shake as "Shook the phone", with no down/up lines. `between(a, b, steps)` builds simulated paths. Every bar gesture is spoken; real pointers are not.
- **Drag targets** are `[data-drop="<row name>"]` rows (not Tab stops). The handle is `[data-drag]`. Pinch goes to the `[data-pinch]` surface. Shake goes to `[data-shake]` elements through `SHAKE_EVENT` and the `useShake` hook (`src/live/motion.ts`). No device motion sensor is read anywhere.
- Lab models are in `src/labs/pointerVerdicts.ts`. A file named `pointerLabs.ts` would clash with `PointerLabs.tsx` on a case-insensitive disk. The 2.5.4 lab passes only with an Undo button AND shaking that can be turned off ("on, with a setting" or "turned off").
- The 2.5.1 fail caption is now "Zooming is gesture-only." The live map has no rotate, so the old caption wasn't true of it.

## Deliberately left
- No real multi-touch e2e (Playwright's mouse is one pointer). The pinch handler is exercised by the bar's synthetic two-finger events.
- The real-pointer recorder hit-tests Tab stops only, so a real drag on the Fixed list says "Pointer down on nothing" for the handle. The reorder itself works and is asserted.
