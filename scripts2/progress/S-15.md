# S-15 — Post: Keyboard accessibility

**Issue:** #83  **Commit:** see the PR (branch `post/s-15-keyboard`)  **Status:** shipped, waiting on the owner's approval of its PR

## What shipped
One post, `src/blog/posts/keyboard-accessibility.md` (`/blog/keyboard-accessibility/`),
published 2026-11-28, about 1,850 words of prose. It follows a keyboard user down a page:
reaching every control (2.1.1), leaving it again (2.1.2), the order focus moves in (2.4.3),
seeing focus (2.4.7, with 1.4.11 for the indicator's contrast and 2.4.13 as the AAA target), and
focus hidden under sticky content (2.4.11). It ends with a seven-step Tab-key check. It embeds
the 2.1.1 examples (`::example{num="2.1.1"}`), the `keyboard-trap` lab for 2.1.2 and the
`focus-ring` lab for 2.4.7.

## Decisions made
- Slug `keyboard-accessibility`: the topic's plain name, not tied to a version or year.
- Criteria: 2.1.1, 2.1.2, 2.4.3, 2.4.7, 2.4.11, 1.4.11. 1.4.11 is there because the post states
  the 3:1 focus-indicator rule and links its page; the standard asks for every criterion named
  to be linked and listed.
- 2.4.13 Focus Appearance (asked for in the issue) is Level AAA and not in the manifest, so it
  has no page here. The post covers it and links W3C's Understanding page instead. 2.1.3 and
  2.4.12 are named the same way.
- No overlap with S-13 (forms): that post left 2.1.1, 2.4.3 and 2.4.7 to this one, and this post
  does not touch labels, errors or autocomplete.
- Contrast figures (#1d4ed8 on #ffffff 6.7:1, on #1e3a8a 1.55:1) computed with the WCAG
  relative-luminance formula.
- Facts checked against the W3C Understanding pages for 2.1.1, 2.1.3, 2.1.2, 2.4.3, 2.4.7,
  2.4.11 and 2.4.13, technique C43, WCAG 2.2 conformance requirement 5 (2.1.2 is one of the four
  non-interference criteria), "What's New in WCAG 2.2", and MDN's `:focus-visible`, `tabindex`
  and `dialog` pages.
- The same two unit-test fixes as S-11 to S-13 (identical, so conflicts resolve to either side):
  `tests/criterionPage.test.tsx` expects "Further reading" when `postsAbout(num, POSTS)` is
  non-empty; `tests/share.test.ts` counts one card per post.
