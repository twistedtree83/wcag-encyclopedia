# L-07 — Focus and hover behaviour: 3.2.1 and 1.4.13

**Issue:** #47  **Commit:** see `git log --grep "L-07:"` on `labs`  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
3.2.1 and 1.4.13 have live examples on both stages and one lab each. 3.2.1: a mock help page with its own address bar and a "Jump to section" select. On Broken, simulated Tab onto the select changes the mock's page at once (and again every time Tab comes back to it); on Fixed, focus does nothing and Go changes the page, then focus moves to the new page's button. The change happens inside the mock only; the real page never navigates. Instruments: a new "Where the example is" history and the focus tracker. 1.4.13: "Estimated total incl. duties" with a tooltip. A new "Hover" key moves a drawn pointer onto the words, into the tooltip, then away, and a new "Escape" key presses Escape. On Broken the tooltip vanishes on the way in and ignores Escape; on Fixed it stays when hovered and Escape closes it. Real mouse, keyboard focus and Escape work on Fixed too. A new "Pointer and tooltip" instrument logs it. Labs: "Try it: what focus does" (what focusing Email in a mock form does: nothing, sends the form, or opens another page, then Tab through the mock) and "Try it: tame the tooltip" (three checkboxes, one per condition, and buttons to hover, move in, wait and press Escape).

## Decisions made
- **Simulated focus fires the example's own handlers:** the fail stage's Tab now dispatches `focusout` on the old stop and `focusin` on the new one (inert blocks real input, not script), so a broken `onFocus` runs as written. Other live examples have no focus handlers, so nothing else changes.
- **Hover:** `SimKey` `hover` and `escape`. Hover targets are `[data-hover="<what the pointer is on>"]`, in DOM order; the pointer moves to the next showing one, then away (`nextHover` in `src/live/pointer.ts`, pure). Moves are dispatched as `mouseout`/`mouseover` pairs with `relatedTarget`, so React's `onMouseEnter`/`onMouseLeave` behave as for a real pointer. Use those, not CSS `:hover`. The bar draws `.sim-cursor` on BOTH stages (a touch reader has no pointer either). Escape is a `keydown` on the simulated/real focused element, else the hovered target, else the example.
- **A document-level Escape listener** in a pass example must ignore untrusted events from outside itself (see `SteadyTip` in `g1-4.tsx`), or the fail stage's bar Escape would close it.
- **What the example shows is observed, not assumed:** one MutationObserver per stage reads open tooltips (`role="tooltip"` not under `[hidden]`) and the mock's page (`[data-context]` text) after any change, pushing `popup` and `context` events. So instruments see outcomes whatever caused them (bar, real mouse, real keys).
- **New `StageEvent`s:** `pointer`, `key`, `popup`, `context` (with `cause`: the reader's last input, `focus` | `action` | `pointer`). On the pass stage only a real Tab key counts as focus, so focus the example moves itself after Go doesn't count. A `context` event restarts the focus tracker (`trackOf`): a new page has its own focus order. The pass stage re-pushes where real focus is after a change of page.
- **Spoken on both stages:** a change of page ("The example moved to …, on focus alone.") and pointer/tooltip outcomes ("Pressed Escape. Tooltip closes."): a mock page or a drawn tooltip reaches no screen reader on its own. Simulated focus on an element that disappears goes nowhere (the drawn ring turns off).
- **Instruments:** `context` ("Where the example is") and `pointer-log` ("Pointer and tooltip", plus "Open now: …"). Pure helpers `contextLog`, `contextSaid`, `pointerLog`, `pointerSaid` in `events.ts`.
- **Labs** keep their own pure models (`onFocus.ts`, `tooltip.ts`) and draw their own focus and pointer in an inert mock; both start failing. No URL state (not flagships).
- The static examples are unchanged. The live 1.4.13 tooltips are placed against the sentence (not the word) so they fit at 320px; the broken one opens 24px below the words, the fixed one touches them.

## Deliberately left
- The drawn pointer is placed when it moves; it does not follow a resize.
- 1.4.13's live examples show dismissable and hoverable; "persistent" (a timer) is shown only in the lab.
- Real pointer events on the pass stage are logged when crossing a `data-hover` target; ordinary mouse movement is not.
