## Decisions made

**Several examples point at the page the reader is standing on.** 2.4.1 invites them to press
Tab and meet the site's own skip link; 2.4.7's diff is the site's actual focus rule verbatim;
2.4.11's diff is the site's actual `scroll-padding-top`. This is the strongest evidence
available that the advice is followed, and it costs nothing to say so.

**2.4.6 is positioned against 1.3.1**, because the pair is genuinely confusing: 1.3.1 asks
whether a heading is *marked up* as a heading, 2.4.6 asks whether it *says* anything. The fail
example is explicit that it passes 1.3.1 and still fails here.

**2.4.4's diff note argues against the popular `aria-label` fix.** Hiding context in an
`aria-label` desynchronises what is seen from what is announced, and voice-control users say
what they see. Rewriting the visible text is the better fix and worth stating, because the
shortcut is what most teams reach for.

**2.4.11's failing example genuinely tucks the field behind the bar** with a negative margin
and `z-index: -1`, rather than describing the effect.
