# T-10 — Demo library index section

**Issue:** #10  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped

The demo library: a grid indexing all nine demos with criterion number, description, and
duration. Authored entries link to the demo in place on its criterion card; the other eight
render as clearly-marked placeholders.

## Decisions made

**Two lists, with different jobs, and a test keeping them honest.** `catalogue.ts` is what the
site *promises* — all nine, with their ids, criteria, and durations. `registry.tsx` is what
*exists*. The library renders the catalogue and checks the registry for each entry, so:

- authoring a demo is **one registry entry** and the index updates itself — there is no second
  list to remember;
- the count in the header ("1 of 9 authored") cannot drift;
- and tests assert the two agree — every registered demo is in the catalogue, and where one is
  authored its duration and criterion match what was promised.

Without that last check a demo could ship at a different length than the library advertises,
which is exactly the sort of small lie that accumulates.

**Placeholders are dashed and labelled "To come"**, not fake play buttons. The count states the
real position rather than implying nine working demos.

**Every entry names its criterion and links by fragment**, so the library and the rail are two
routes to the same card — which is 2.4.5 Multiple Ways, applied to the page itself.

## Deliberately left

The eight remaining timelines — T-11 and T-12. Each needs a file under `src/demo/demos/` and one
entry in `registry.tsx`; the catalogue entry, the library tile, and the count are already
waiting for them.
