# S-02 — Unique titles and descriptions, and a head-metadata test

**Issue:** #70  **Commit:** see `git log --grep "S-02"`  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped
Criterion pages now build their title and description from the record in `src/site/meta.ts`:
"1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) explained, with examples | WCAG 2.2 AA", and a description made of the
restatement in one sentence then "Fails when:" and the fail caption. `tests/headMeta.test.ts`
checks every route in `ROUTES` (what the prerender step writes) for a present, unique title of
30–65 characters and description of 70–160, the brand at most once per title and first on Home,
and no sentence repeated across the 55 criterion descriptions.

## Decisions made
- **Title fallbacks.** Long names don't fit the full form, so `criterionTitle` takes the first of
  `… explained, with examples | WCAG 2.2 <level>`, `… explained | WCAG 2.2 <level>`,
  `… | WCAG 2.2 <level>`, then `<num> <name>` that is at most 65 characters. 1.2.3 is the only
  record on the last form. Criterion titles carry no brand.
- **Gist.** 27 restatements open with a sentence too long to sit beside the fail caption in 160
  characters, so those records carry `gist`, a one-sentence original restatement (new term in
  `CONTEXT.md`, optional field on `CriterionRecord`). The test fails both on a gist that isn't
  needed and on a missing one, so the gist set stays exactly the records that need it.
- Only the restatement's *first* sentence is used: later sentences such as "New in WCAG 2.2."
  repeat across records, which the no-repeated-sentence test catches.
- "Fails when:" follows design guide §11; the caption's first letter is lowered unless it starts
  an acronym or a number.
- About's title was 24 characters; it is now "About the site and its accessibility: WCAG
  Encyclopedia". Other fixed titles and all fixed descriptions already fitted and are unchanged.
- 1.4.10's fail caption lost its inner colon ("Fixed-width columns make the page scroll sideways
  as well as down at 320px wide.") so the description doesn't read "Fails when: …: …".
