      What the numbers mean

      Contrast is measured from 1 : 1, the same colour twice, to 21 : 1, black on white. The rule you need depends on what the colour is doing.

      • 4.5 : 1

        Body text

        Anything people read below large size. Level AA, criterion 1.4.3. At AAA, criterion 1.4.6 asks for 7 : 1.

      • 3 : 1

        Large text

        At least 24px, or 18.66px when bold. Bigger letters stay readable with less contrast.

      • 3 : 1

        Controls and graphics

        Field borders, focus rings, icons and chart lines, measured against what sits next to them. Criterion 1.4.11.

      What a ratio can’t tell you

      Passing the number is the minimum. These still need a human look.

      • Thin and small fontsA hairline weight at 4.5 : 1 can still be hard to read. The ratio assumes ordinary strokes.
      • Text over imagesA photo has hundreds of background colours. Check the lightest part behind the text, or add a solid backing.
      • Hover, focus and disabled statesEach state is a new pair of colours. Check them all, not just the resting one.
      • Colour as the only signalTwo colours can both pass and still be told apart only by hue. Criterion 1.4.1 asks for a second cue, such as an icon or underline.
