Specimen 1.4.11

Non-text contrast

Success Criterion 1.4.11, Level AA

With a minimum of 3 : 1 against whatever sits next to it, every part of a control you need to see must stand apart: the edge of a text field, the state of a checkbox, the ring that shows where focus is. Text has its own rule. This one covers everything else that carries meaning, including the lines and wedges of a chart.

Most often found in forms. Text fields drawn with pale hairlines, checkboxes that turn a slightly different grey when ticked, and icon-only buttons.

Also common in charts, where two lines are told apart by colour alone.
