      Three contrast rules

      People often treat contrast as one number. WCAG has three, and they measure different things.

      • 1.4.3 · Level AA

        Text

        4.5 : 1

        Body text against its background. Large text, 24px or 19px bold, needs 3 : 1.

      • 1.4.6 · Level AAA

        Enhanced text

        7 : 1

        The stricter version, for people with moderately low vision who don't use magnification.

      • 1.4.11 · Level AA

        Controls & graphics

        3 : 1

        Field borders, focus rings, icons and chart lines against whatever is next to them.
